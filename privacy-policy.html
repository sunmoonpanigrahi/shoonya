Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Find with Sadhguru is an independent discovery app that helps people find public content on official Isha and Sadhguru pages. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Isha Foundation or Sadhguru.

Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics. Search terms are used on the device to find matching catalog entries. The app downloads public catalog metadata from its configured hosting address and stores a local copy so the catalog remains available offline.

If you choose voice search, Android opens the device's speech-recognition service. That service may process your voice according to the provider's own privacy policy. The app does not record or store audio.

Links to other services

Search results open the original public pages on Isha or Sadhguru channels. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies.

Children and data deletion

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from users. Since the app does not maintain user accounts or transmit search histories, there is no app-held personal search data to delete.
